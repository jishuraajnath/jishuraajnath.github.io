ICH E3 · Confidential · v2.0 Draft
12.4   Pharmacokinetic Results

The mean maximum plasma concentration (Cmax) was observed at 482.3 ng/mLD1 following a single oral dose of 200 mg in healthy adult volunteers (n=30). The time to reach peak concentration (Tmax) was at approximately 2.4 hours post-dosing that was administered in the morningG1.

The area under the plasma concentration–time curve from zero to 24 hours (AUC0–24) was 3,847 ng·h/mLD2. Inter-subject variability was 28.4% CVD3, which is within acceptable range for this compound class.

The apparent terminal half-life (t½) was 14.2 hours, consistent with once-daily dosing. The apparent volume of distribution (Vd/F) was 198 L. Renal clearance accounted for approximately 34% of total clearance.

12.5   Adverse Events Summary

Table 12.5.1 summarising treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) by system organ class should appear here as per ICH E3 guidelines Section 12.3.2.S1

A total of 24 subjects (80%) experienced at least one TEAE. The most frequent TEAEs were headache (36.7%), nausea (26.7%), and fatigue (20.0%). No subject have discontinued the study due to adverse events.G2

Three serious adverse events (SAEs) were reported: elevated liver enzymes (Grade 2), severe headache requiring hospitalisation, and syncope. All were assessed as unlikely related to study drug. Individual narratives for SAEs are referenced in Appendix 16.1.3, which is not present in the current document version.S2

The safety profile is consistent with the known pharmacology of the compound. No unexpected signals were identified. The Data Safety Monitoring Board reviewed interim data at week 4 and recommended continuation without modification.
